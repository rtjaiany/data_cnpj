## Reconstruction method

The extract was generated by a targeted chronological replay for the 134,345 cohort keys only. It did not export or create a full national monthly panel.

Events were applied in chronological order from August 2023 through October 2024:

- `INSERT` creates national presence;
- `UPDATE` replaces the establishment state with `conteudo_novo`;
- `DELETE` removes national presence;
- when an early candidate first appeared as an `UPDATE`, its prior state was initialized from `conteudo_anterior`.

This forward replay was chosen over reverse reconstruction because it follows the established temporal logic and avoids having to invert `conteudo_anterior`/`conteudo_novo` semantics.

## Additional finding about the 134,343/134,345 discrepancy

The chronological event audit found:

- 134,343 candidates whose first targeted establishment event is an `INSERT`;
- 2 candidates whose first targeted event is an `UPDATE`.

This is a useful lead for explaining the historical 134,343 count, but it is not sufficient evidence to remove those two establishments from the frozen population. The delivered extract therefore retains all 134,345 reproducible candidates.
